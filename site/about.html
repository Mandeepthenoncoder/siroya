Zaveri BazaarMumbai
1950

Where it all began

Bastimal Siroya set up a small silver jewellery unit in Zaveri Bazaar, Mumbai. It began modestly, and the work was done by hand.

In 1950, Bastimal Siroya, father of our co-founder Dheerajmal Siroya, set up a small silver jewellery unit in Mumbai's Zaveri Bazaar. It began modestly, with no big investment and no flashy storefront.

In those early years, the family worked mainly in silver. The work was done by hand, and it soon drew attention in India and beyond.

Our family's roots are Rajasthani, a heritage that still inspires our Rangmahal collection. Everything that followed began in that one unit.

Silver to goldLocal and export markets
1970s

From silver to gold

Trust in the work kept growing. By the 1970s the family had moved from silver to gold and stepped into larger markets.

By the 1970s, the family had moved from silver to gold. Customers had come to trust the work, and that trust inspired the move as the business stepped into larger markets.

The Zaveri Bazaar unit grew into a business known to local and export buyers alike. What never changed was the heart behind the work.

What came next would take the family's craft across the sea, to the UAE.

SharjahUnited Arab Emirates
1974

A factory in Sharjah

In 1974, with no jewellery factories in sight, our family opened one in Sharjah. It began with 25 craftsmen and a small monthly output.

In 1974, our family saw an opportunity in the UAE. With no jewellery factories in sight, they opened one in Sharjah. It started with a team of 25 craftsmen and a small monthly output.

The factory traded as Shattaf Jewellers. Its output soon grew to thousands of kilograms a month, and Shattaf became a preferred name in the Gulf.

1992

The Siroya brand is born

In 1992, our family opened a wholesale office in Deira Gold Souk, Dubai. It was the birth of Siroya as a brand.

In 1992, Siroya became a brand in its own right. Stepping away from Shattaf, our family opened its first wholesale office in the busy Deira Gold Souk, Dubai. This is why we call ourselves a family business established in 1992, with a craft that is much older.

From Deira we served wholesale clients across Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain and the UAE. They came to know Siroya for two things: dependable quality and the ability to deliver at scale.

Other jewellers placed their trust in us, and we still work to keep it.

2001

Our first store

In 2001 we opened our first retail store in Meena Bazaar, a place whose warmth and bustle reminded the family of Zaveri Bazaar.

In 2001, it was time to bring the Siroya name directly to customers. Our first retail store opened in Meena Bazaar, Dubai's shopping district for the Indian community.

We chose Meena Bazaar for a reason. Its bustle, warmth and community spirit were much like those of Zaveri Bazaar, where the family's story began in 1950. It felt like home.

The store brought Kundan and antique-style jewellery to Dubai's Indian families. This is when Siroya went from wholesale to homes and became your family jewellers too. In time, more stores followed across Dubai.

2015

A Guinness World Record

The world's longest gold chain, handmade and 5,522 metres long, was unveiled in Dubai. Siroya is proud to be part of that Guinness World Record.

In 2015, the world's longest handmade gold chain was unveiled in Dubai. It measured 5,522 metres from end to end, more than five kilometres of gold.

Siroya is proud to be part of that Guinness World Record. It honours something our family has always valued: handwork, done patiently and done well.

A record is a rare moment, but it is not why we do this work. We make jewellery for the special days in a family's life.

2020

Evermore, our lab-grown diamonds

Our customers wanted diamonds with brilliance and no compromise. In 2020 we launched Evermore, our lab-grown diamond brand.

Our customers had a new wish: diamonds that offered brilliance without compromise. In 2020 we launched Evermore, our lab-grown diamond brand.

Evermore has since become a trusted name for lab-grown diamond jewellery in Dubai, with its own boutique in the city.

Lab-grown diamonds may seem a long way from a silver unit in Zaveri Bazaar. We see the same habit at work: listen to families, then make what they are looking for, with care. Rooted in heritage. Inspired by the future.

14x

Honoured along the way

We have been named Highest Importer of Gold Jewellery from India 14 times, and GJEPC has honoured us for our contribution to the industry.

Siroya has won the Highest Importer of Gold Jewellery from India award 14 times. We are proud of each one.

India's Gem and Jewellery Export Promotion Council (GJEPC) has also recognised Siroya for its lifetime contribution to the industry. A visit from Mr Ratan Tata to our family's first factory remains one of our proudest moments.

Through all of it, families have kept coming back to us, generation after generation.

Today

Your family jewellers

Today we have four stores in Dubai, a wholesale business that supplies jewellers in 33 countries, and the same family care that began in Zaveri Bazaar.

Our wholesale business now supplies jewellers in 33 countries and 6,000 retailers worldwide.

We have four stores in Dubai: Meena Bazaar, Al Karama, Al Qusais and Deira. The Siroya House of Brands includes Rangmahal, Sanskriti, Prestige and Evermore.

We are still a family business, and our long-standing team is part of that family. We began in 1950 with a small silver unit in Mumbai. Today, we are proud to be your family jewellers.
